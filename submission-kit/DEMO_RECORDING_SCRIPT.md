## 🎙️ Recording Best Practices
1. **Pacing**: Speak at a measured, confident tempo (~130 words per minute).
2. **Cursor Motion**: Smooth, intentional cursor paths with smooth clicks (no rapid twitching).
3. **Format**: H.264 MP4 format, AAC audio, 1080p resolution. Keep file size under 50 MB to easily attach to social media or GitHub.
