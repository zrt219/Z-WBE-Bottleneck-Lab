What does Amdahl's Law really teach us when applied to whole-brain simulation?

Amdahl's Law states that the overall speedup of a system is limited by the fraction of the system that cannot be accelerated.

In biological simulation:
- If synaptic state lookups occupy 60% of total cycle time, making neural soma computation 1000x faster yields less than a 2.5x overall pipeline speedup.
- Memory bandwidth saturation becomes the asymptotic ceiling.
