Whole-Brain Emulation cannot be treated as isolated academic silos.

If a team solves imaging without modeling memory bandwidth, the project hits a wall. If they solve compute without calculating power dissipation, the cluster cannot be cooled.

Z-WBE models the entire macroscopic pipeline as 6 interdependent stages:
1. Preservation: Vitrification / chemical stabilization and volumetric retention.
2. Acquisition: Multi-beam SEM imaging throughput and isotropic voxel rates.
3. Reconstruction: Deep-learning segmentation, skeletonization, and proofreading.
4. Functionalization: Inferring neurotransmitter receptors and physiological channel dynamics.
5. Execution: Real-time biophysical simulation of 86B neurons and 150T synapses.
6. Validation: Cross-scale causal verification, stimulus-response matching, and functional fidelity.

Each stage is bound by 8 physical constraints: Acquisition Time, Reconstruction Compute, Memory Bandwidth, Storage Volume, Interconnect Fabric, Thermal Power, Economic Cost, and Scientific Uncertainty.
