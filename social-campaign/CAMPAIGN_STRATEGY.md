# Z-WBE 21-Day Launch Sprint Campaign Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

**Z-WBE Bottleneck Lab** is an interactive systems-modeling demonstrator engineered for the **Google Cloud × NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 Golden Ticket Challenge**. 

The 21-day maximum-exposure campaign operates as an evidence-first, build-in-public launch sprint across **LinkedIn** and **X (Twitter)**. Its mission is to make the engineering realities of Whole Brain Emulation (WBE):
- **IMPOSSIBLE TO MISUNDERSTAND**
- **DIFFICULT TO FORGET**
- **EASY TO TRY**
- **EASY TO VERIFY**
- **EASY TO REPRODUCE**

---

## 2. Campaign Rhythm & Cadence

- **Duration**: 21 consecutive days (September 8, 2026 – September 28, 2026)
- **Timezone**: Mountain Daylight Time (MDT / UTC-6)
- **Daily Cadence**: 5–6 posts/day on LinkedIn and 5–6 posts/day on X

- **Contest Deadline Focus**: Days 1–3 (September 8–10, 2026) are flagged as high-priority contest submission windows with explicit tags for `@GoogleDevs`, `@NVIDIAAI`, `Jen Harvey`, and `Ray Harvey`.

## 5. Media Rules & Asset Sequencing

Per Buffer API specifications, posts may never mix media types:
- **Multi-Image Carousel (3–4 images)**:
  - Image 1: High-impact marketing cover (`public/marketing/ad_01.png` or `public/images/banner-dark.png`)
  - Image 2: Real application UI state (`public/screenshots/01_hero_overview.png`)
  - Image 3: Real benchmark / terminal proof (`public/colab-evidence/05_colab_nvidia_smi_ensemble_eval.png`)
  - Image 4: Result chart, badge, or call-to-action (`public/data/cpu_vs_gpu_speedup.png` or `public/images/google-nvidia-developer-badges.png`)
- **Video Post**: Single MP4 file with clear branded first frame (`public/recordings/hero_bottleneck_shift.mp4`, etc.).
- **GIF Post**: Single animated GIF file (`public/recordings/hero_bottleneck_shift.gif`, `public/recordings/colab_t4_live_execution.gif`, etc.).

## 7. Performance & Grill-Me Learning Loop

Every 48 hours, campaign metrics are evaluated against the following criteria:
1. **Grill-Me Verification**: Every quantitative claim is cross-checked against `CLAIM_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md`. If a claim lacks proof, publication is halted.
2. **Hook Diagnostics**: Top 5 performing hooks are modeled for subsequent weeks; low-performing openings are rewritten.
3. **Follow-up Branching**: If a post generates high engagement (e.g., "The Bottleneck Moved"), dedicated follow-up micro-threads are deployed to unpack secondary implications.
