## 2. Platform-Specific Usage Rules

### X (Twitter) Rule
- **Rule**: Exactly 1 to 2 hashtags per post.
- **Formula**: `#NVIDIAGTC` + [One context-specific tag].
- **Rationale**: Character economy and algorithm optimization. Dense hashtag blocks depress engagement on X.

### LinkedIn Rule
- **Rule**: Exactly 5 to 8 focused hashtags per post.
- **Placement**: Clean block at the bottom of the post, preceded by a blank line.
- **Selection**: Combine 1 contest tag + 2 hardware/cloud tags + 2 domain tags + 1 engineering tag.
